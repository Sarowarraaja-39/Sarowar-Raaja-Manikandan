Included editable source path: react-source/public/assets/sarowar-avatar-v1.png
The portable browser and static-hosting builds include the same transparent asset.

Method: built-in image_gen tool, not CLI fallback. Identity reference: the user-supplied IMG_1832.jpg. The original upload is unchanged. The PNG retains its alpha channel. Animation comes from CSS in src/styles/poster.css: a gentle nod/rock and a rotating dashed orbit, with a separate arrow badge. It stops with the page motion toggle or system reduced-motion preference.

Final generation prompt
-----------------------
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: small personal avatar for a professional portfolio contact button. Input image is an identity reference of Sarowar, the man in the navy suit. Create one polished, friendly 3D clay-style head-and-shoulders avatar of this same man, keeping his recognizable South Asian face, warm medium-brown skin, thick wavy dark hair with subtle grey at the temples, short beard and moustache, and navy suit over a white open-collar shirt. Slight welcoming smile, looking toward camera, front-facing, head slightly larger than realistic for legibility at 80px. Frame as a compact bust, entire hair and shoulders visible with padding. Warm studio lighting with a gentle amber rim light, restrained premium character style, crisp clean silhouette. Truly transparent background with real alpha, no coloured circle, no environment, no text, no logo lettering, no arrow, no watermark, no props, just the single avatar. It will sit inside an amber circle and receive a gentle nod animation in CSS. Preserve identity from the reference photo.
